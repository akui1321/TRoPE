TRoPE reduces sticking artifacts in video-to-4D generation by making mesh connectivity explicit in motion retrieval. It helps nearby parts follow their own motion and separate after contact.

Results

Nine examples across human and other articulated geometries. The four videos in each example play together; the reconstructed meshes share the same camera.
